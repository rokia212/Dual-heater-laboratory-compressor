Project Overview

The Dual Heater Laboratory Compressor is a sustainable air compression system built from recycled electronic waste, designed to reduce environmental impact while providing reliable laboratory performance.

How It Works

Stage 1:
Component Extraction Extract the refrigerator motor and freon container as primary parts, then salvage thermal components from a hairdryer to enable air heating. Key regulation tools like the pressure switch and check valve are gathered from auto spare parts.
Stage 2:
Component Integration The motor is fixed onto a drilled base, connecting its suction part to a check valve and its delivery part to the freon tank. The tank is then fitted with a pressure regulator and switch to manage air flow and prevent leaks.
Stage 3:
Electrical Wiring Wiring is centralized in the pressure switch, connecting the motor and heating elements to the AC power source. This setup ensures that current flows correctly to both the dryer and motor, allowing for synchronized operation.
